# Songr 1.4.5

This release fixes navigation and playback-bar issues, polishes the Library's
alphabet index, and fixes artwork display.

- Browser **Back** from an album now returns to its artist every time, not only
  the first time after you navigate in and out.
- When Songr opens before Roon has finished connecting, the page it opens on now
  loads by itself as soon as Roon is ready, without switching tabs.
- The playback bar shows a track's artists on one line instead of cutting every
  name short. Hover over the line, or move keyboard focus to it, to read the
  whole credit; with reduced motion on, the complete list appears above it and
  every artist in it can be clicked.
- In Safari, album frames and glow in the **Laser**, **Miami** and **Pop Art**
  themes are no longer cut off at the edges of large album grids.
- The A–Z index now fits the window instead of scrolling whenever there is
  room. Letters magnify gently under the pointer (not when reduced motion is
  on), touch screens keep large targets, and keyboard focus is clearly visible.
- **Laser**, **Miami** and **Pop Art** use neutral scrollbars instead of their
  bright border colour.
- With reduced motion turned on, in Songr or in your system settings, album
  artwork appears faster in large libraries.

Known limitation: in Safari, very large libraries (around 40,000 albums) can
freeze for a minute or longer after the Albums tab is first shown. Libraries of
a few thousand albums are not affected, and other browsers are not affected.

Application identity, pairing and saved settings are retained when upgrading.
