# Songr 1.4.4

This release adds three color themes and a new way to group your albums.

- Choose **Laser**, **Miami** or **Pop Art** in **Settings → Appearance → Theme**,
  alongside Dark and Light. Your choice is saved on this device and applied
  immediately when Songr opens.
- Choose **Group by Artist** in the Albums sort menu to put each artist's albums
  together. **Group by Letter** remains available; grouping preferences persist
  across reloads, and the alphabet rail follows the selected grouping.
- Long artist headings stay on one line. Hover or tap to scroll the full name
  at a reading pace; reduced motion shows the complete name in a stationary popup.
- Retry a failed album Play action directly from its error message. Opening
  More preserves the failed action so Retry keeps the original Play intent.
- Keep Browse More controls in sync with available actions as the page changes.

Application identity, pairing and saved settings are retained when upgrading.
