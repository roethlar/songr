# Songr 1.4.3

This release improves library browsing and track selection, replaces Songr Favorites
with Bookmarks, and adds update checks.

- Tap or click tracks to select them; tap again to deselect. Selected titles turn
  Songr gold. Playback actions, Select all and Clear appear with a selection,
  without moving the track list. Multi-track actions follow the displayed order.
- Save tracks, albums and artists in **Bookmarks**, separate from Roon Favorites.
  Existing Songr favorites are retained, and the 500-item limit is removed.
- Choose Icons, Text or Both for actions, plus smooth scrolling and interface
  motion preferences in Settings. These preferences and pinned navigation pages
  are saved on the connected server and shared by its clients.
- Browse Tracks, Composers and Tags with filtering, sorting and continuous lists.
  Open tagged albums and artists in their normal detail pages; composers lead to
  compositions and recordings. Large track-list preparation yields between
  cancellable batches to keep the interface responsive.
- Keep Artists grouped alphabetically by default; albums and tracks are ungrouped.
  Preserve numbering supplied by Roon without inventing list-position numbers.
- Improve artwork loading and recovery, fix clipped artwork borders and nested
  navigation, and fix the macOS startup window error.
- Remove Track Info and unsupported public-API actions. Playlists stay hidden
  because supported playlists cannot be reliably distinguished.
- Use **About → Check for updates** to check the connected server. The desktop
  app also checks its own Songr version at startup. Both link to a newer release;
  neither installs updates automatically.

Application identity, pairing and saved settings are retained when upgrading.
Live radio remains experimental and playback has not been verified; feedback is
welcome. Refresh Songr after changing tags in Roon.
